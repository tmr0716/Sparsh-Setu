Who We Are

A Student-Driven Digital Inclusion Movement

Sparsh Setu (meaning "The Touch of Connection") was established as an internship initiative dedicated to bridging the digital divide in underserved communities.

Many young students in government and local schools lack access to foundational computer education. Our team organizes hands-on workshops, installs educational reference materials, and mentors children on digital navigation, online safety, and modern tools.
