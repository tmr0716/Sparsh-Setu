Our Purpose

Bridging the Digital Gap

Every child deserves equal access to digital tools and safety awareness in today's interconnected world.

Our Mission

To eliminate digital illiteracy among underprivileged school children by providing direct, practical access to computer basics, keyboard literacy, software navigation, and internet safety education.

Our Vision

A future where no student is left behind due to a lack of technology access, creating a tech-savvy generation confident in using digital tools for higher education and career development.

What We Do

Core Internship Highlights

A brief look at the core activities executed during our internship program.

Computer Basics

Introducing hardware, operating system navigation, basic keyboard shortcuts, and simple document creation.

Cyber Safety

Educating children on secure browsing practices, identifying phishing attempts, and maintaining password safety.

Poster Campaign

Designing and installing visual educational posters across school labs to reinforce key computing principles daily.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Sparsh Setu?

Sparsh Setu is a digital literacy initiative established as an internship drive to bring foundational computing knowledge and internet safety awareness to school students.

How can I volunteer?

You can sign up directly via our Volunteer page form. We welcome college students, tech professionals, and educators to mentor upcoming drives.

Does the AI Chatbot require internet API tokens?

No, Sparsh Assist runs entirely on our self-contained Flask rule engine, delivering instant responses without third-party key requirements.
